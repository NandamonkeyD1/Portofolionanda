Case Study 05 // UI/UX & Product Design

Enterprise Dashboard & Data Analytics

A multi-tier data management interface designed in Figma with verified visual hierarchies, ergonomic business metrics, and high administrative efficiency.

01 / Project Overview

This UI/UX design initiative focused on synthesizing complex operational streams—transaction volumes, conversion cohorts, and infrastructure health logs—into an uncluttered control interface. Core design criteria prioritized dark-theme ocular comfort, high information density without visual crowding, and standardized component modularity.

02 / Problem & Motivation

Legacy analytical consoles often suffer from cognitive overload, forcing operators to parse unformatted numerical data tables. The objective was to decrease mean time-to-insight (MTTI) through contextual spatial clustering and intentional chromatic alerts.

03 / My Role & Contributions

As the UI/UX Designer & Product Prototyper:

  • Investigated operational behavioral patterns among data administrators during high-volume work shifts.
  • Created a full-scale Design System in Figma (color tokens, auto-layout typography scale, icon library, and reusable component variants).
  • Engineered modular KPI summary cards (revenue velocity, retention percentage, server log status).
  • Built interactive high-fidelity prototypes to test navigation ergonomics and multi-filter criteria.

04 / Technical Stack

Figma Component Variants & Auto-Layout Interactive Prototyping Information Architecture WCAG 2.1 Contrast Standards

05 / Design & Architecture Decisions

Built atop a responsive 12-column layout grid featuring collapsible navigation. Data charts employ functional chromatic semantics: cobalt blue designates primary volumes, purple indicates projection curves, while neon lime and warning vermilion highlight normal versus critical alert states.

06 / Screenshots & Implementation Evidence

Preview of the analytical card modularity, filter breadcrumbs, and data density balance.

07 / Challenges & Lessons Learned

The primary challenge was balancing maximum information density with visual whitespace to prevent eye strain during long operational shifts. We resolved this through restrained 1px surface borders (12% opacity) and geometric sans-serif typefaces with generous character spacing.

08 / Outcome & Project Status

The completed Figma prototype passed usability verification and was adopted as the foundational interface design benchmark for internal software products at Solvia Nova.
